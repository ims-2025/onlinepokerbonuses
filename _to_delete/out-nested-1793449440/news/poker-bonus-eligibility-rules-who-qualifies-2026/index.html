Poker Bonus Eligibility: Who Actually Qualifies in 2026

Welcome offers are not open to everyone. Learn the common eligibility rules on US and Canada poker sites, from new-account status to residency and codes.

OnlinePokerBonuses Editorial Team·Published October 31, 2026·7 min read

A big welcome offer is only valuable if you actually qualify for it. Every year, players deposit expecting a headline bonus and discover afterward that something in their account history, location or deposit method made them ineligible. Most of these disappointments are avoidable, because eligibility rules at online poker rooms follow a handful of predictable patterns.

This guide walks through the most common conditions attached to poker bonuses on sites serving US and Canadian players, so you can check your eligibility before you put money down. Always confirm the current details on the room's own promotions page, since terms change, and use our best poker bonuses rankings as a starting point for comparison.

New-account status

Welcome bonuses are designed to attract new customers, and most rooms define a new customer narrowly. In general, you must not have held an account with the same room before, and in many cases you must not have held one at a sister site that shares the same network or operator. The rule exists to stop the same player from collecting a first-deposit offer repeatedly under different names.

That matters because several of the rooms we cover share infrastructure. Americas Cardroom and Black Chip Poker run on the WPN network, while BetOnline, TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag run on the Chico network. Depending on the promotion, a prior account at one room on a network may or may not affect eligibility at another. Our explainer on sister sites and network bonuses covers how to think about this, but the safest approach is to read the terms of the specific offer.

Residency and location

Offers are typically tied to where you live and where you are connecting from. Many promotions are open to players in the United States and Canada, but individual rooms can exclude particular states or provinces, and some offers are restricted to specific regions or currencies. A promotion that exists for one country may not be available in another, even on the same site.

Location checks are also technical. Rooms use geolocation and IP data to confirm where you are playing, and using a VPN can cause problems that include flagged accounts and withheld rewards. See our piece on VPNs and geolocation risks before you consider anything that masks your location. If you want to understand the legal landscape for your state, check our USA hub or the Canada hub.

Age and identity requirements

To claim any offer, you must meet the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction, which varies by state and province, and you must be able to verify your identity. Verification is usually required before a withdrawal, but some rooms request documents earlier, for example if an account triggers a review. Details you provide must match your documents exactly, including your name and address.

If you plan to cash out soon, completing verification early prevents delays. Our guide to KYC verification before cashing out explains which documents are commonly requested and how to prepare them.

Bonus codes and how you sign up

Many offers require a bonus code at registration or at the first deposit. If you forget to enter it, or enter it after the deposit, the room may be unable to apply the bonus retroactively. Some promotions are credited automatically, while others require you to opt in through the cashier. The terms for the specific offer will tell you which approach applies.

Entering the code correctly is one of the simplest ways to protect your eligibility. Our how to claim guide walks through the typical steps, and our bonus codes guide explains how codes work and why they are tied to specific offers.

Deposit method and minimums

A bonus can depend on how you fund your account. Some offers require a minimum deposit, and some boost the match for certain payment methods, such as crypto. Others exclude particular methods from the promotion entirely, so a deposit made the wrong way can fail to qualify. The details differ from room to room, and they can change when promotions are refreshed.

  • Check the minimum qualifying deposit and the maximum bonus before you fund the account.
  • Confirm whether your payment method is eligible for the full offer or an alternative boosted match.
  • Look for single-deposit rules, since splitting a deposit into several smaller payments can affect the match.
  • Note any time limit on making the first deposit after registration.

If you are choosing between payment options, our analysis of crypto deposit boosts and crypto versus card deposits can help you decide which method fits your plan.

One account per person and household rules

Most rooms limit each player to a single account, and many restrict promotions to one per household, device or shared network. If you live with another poker player, or share a connection with one, the room may flag both accounts for review. This is a security measure rather than a penalty, but it can freeze rewards while the review is underway.

If this applies to you, contact support before depositing and explain your situation. Having a record of the response protects you if something goes wrong later. For a broader look at what rooms monitor, read our guide on how rooms detect bonus abuse.

Playing requirements and expiry

Eligibility does not end at the deposit. Most welcome bonuses release gradually as you generate rake or earn points, and they usually carry an expiry window. If you do not meet the playing requirement in time, the unreleased portion can be forfeited. Games that count toward the requirement can also differ, with some formats contributing less than others.

Plan your volume realistically before you accept. Our guides on bonus expiry dates and how long it takes to clear a bonus by stakes show how to match an offer to the volume you can actually play.

A quick eligibility checklist

  1. Confirm you have no prior account at the room, or a related room, that could disqualify you.
  2. Verify your state or province is covered by the offer.
  3. Check that your age and identity documents are ready.
  4. Find the correct bonus code and enter it at the right step.
  5. Pick an eligible payment method and meet the minimum deposit.
  6. Read the playing requirement and expiry window and make sure you can meet them.

What to do if you think you qualify but did not get the bonus

Contact live support right away, preferably before you play many hands. Provide the account name, deposit time and code you used. Keep screenshots of the offer page, since terms can change. Many cases are resolved quickly when the evidence is clear. Our guide on what to do when a bonus is not credited walks through the steps.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a welcome bonus if I had an account before?

Usually not. Most welcome offers are limited to new customers, and some also exclude players with accounts at related sister sites. Check the terms of the specific offer.

Does my deposit method affect bonus eligibility?

Often yes. Some offers require a minimum deposit or boost the match for certain methods like crypto, while others exclude some methods, so confirm before you fund your account.
