TigerGaming vs SportsBetting.ag: Chico Value

They share the same Chico Network player pool, so what actually separates TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag for bonus value?

OnlinePokerBonuses Editorial Team·Published August 5, 2026·8 min read

Here's a question that trips up a lot of US and Canada players: if TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag share the same poker network, does it actually matter which one you choose? Both rooms run on the Chico Network, which means they share the same tables, the same tournament schedule and the same player pool. Sit down at either and you could be playing against the very same opponents. Yet the two are not interchangeable, and the differences that remain are worth understanding before you decide where to put a deposit. This is the kind of comparison our best poker bonuses rankings are built on.

In this analysis we'll separate what's genuinely shared from what actually differs, so you can pick the Chico skin that fits your game rather than the one with the loudest banner.

What the two rooms share

Start with the shared foundation, because it's substantial. Because TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag both draw from the same Chico liquidity, the two rooms offer:

  • The same player pool and table traffic — cash game and tournament fields are identical across both skins.
  • The same tournament schedule — the guarantees, series and Sunday majors are network-wide events you can enter from either room.
  • The same software and client — the poker engine, hand history and interface are functionally the same experience.
  • Comparable banking rails — both lean heavily on crypto for fast deposits and withdrawals, alongside traditional methods.

That shared core is important context. The choice between them is not a choice between two different games — it's a choice between two front doors to the same room. So the deciding factors come down to the wrapper: the welcome bonus terms, the rakeback, the promotions, and the wider platform each brand sits inside.

The welcome bonus comparison

Both rooms greet poker players with a match-style welcome bonus that releases the familiar way — incrementally, as you generate rake and tournament fees, rather than as instant cash. The headline structures are broadly similar, which is unsurprising given the shared network economics. What you should compare closely is the release rate and the clearing window rather than the top-line percentage, because those details determine how much of the match you'll actually collect. If point-based clearing is new to you, our guide to how poker bonuses work explains the mechanics that apply to both rooms.

The practical takeaway: don't assume the room with the bigger banner number is the better value. Two offers with identical headline percentages can differ meaningfully once you account for how fast the bonus unlocks and how long you have to unlock it. Read the terms on each before you deposit, and time your deposit to a busy stretch so the match clears efficiently.

Where the platforms diverge

The most meaningful difference between these two brands isn't in the poker room at all — it's in everything around it. SportsBetting.ag is, as the name suggests, built with a heavy sportsbook and casino emphasis, and it shares infrastructure with the broader BetOnline family. TigerGaming positions itself as a more poker-and-casino-forward brand with its own promotional cadence. For a player who wants a single account spanning poker, sports and casino, that surrounding ecosystem can tip the decision.

This matters for cross-product promotions. If you also bet sports or play casino games, the room whose wider promotions you'll actually use delivers more total value than a marginally better poker headline on a platform whose other products you'll ignore. A pure poker grinder can weigh the two rooms almost entirely on poker terms; a mixed-action player should factor in the whole platform.

Rakeback and ongoing value

Because both rooms sit on Chico, their ongoing rakeback and loyalty mechanics are similar in structure — a percentage of your rake returned as you play, layered on top of any welcome or reload match you're clearing. As always, this rakeback runs in parallel with your welcome bonus, so on either room you're earning two value streams at once during your early volume. The comparison here is less about which room pays more and more about which brand's recurring promotions align with how and when you play.

For a fuller picture of how the Chico rooms stack up against their WPN counterparts like Americas Cardroom and BetOnline, it's worth reading a cross-network comparison — but between TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag specifically, the rakeback question is close to a wash, and the tie-breakers lie elsewhere.

So which should you choose?

Because the poker itself is identical, the honest answer is that you should choose on the wrapper. Pick the room whose welcome-bonus release rate and clearing window suit your volume, whose surrounding platform matches the other products you actually use, and whose ongoing promotions fit your schedule. If you're a poker-only player, either room gives you the same games — so go with the better-timed, better-structured bonus. If you play sports or casino too, let the wider ecosystem break the tie.

One more consideration for US and Canada players: legality and banking are network-level rather than brand-level, so both rooms serve the same regions with the same crypto-first cashout experience. Our USA and Canada guides cover the regional picture in depth.

The bottom line

TigerGaming and SportsBetting.ag are two doors into the same Chico Network room, so the choice between them is a choice of wrapper, not of game. Compare release rates and clearing windows rather than headline percentages, factor in whichever surrounding platform you'll actually use, and remember that rakeback stacks on top on either brand. Get those details right and it genuinely won't matter which door you walked through — you'll be getting the network's full value either way.
