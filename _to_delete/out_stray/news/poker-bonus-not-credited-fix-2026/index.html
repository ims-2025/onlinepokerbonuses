Poker Bonus Not Credited? How to Get It Fixed

Deposited and the bonus never showed up? Here is why poker bonuses fail to credit, what to check first, and how to get support to resolve it fast.

OnlinePokerBonuses Editorial Team·Published September 18, 2026·9 min read

You funded your account, you expected a pending bonus balance to appear alongside it, and there is nothing there. It is one of the most common frustrations in online poker, and it is also one of the most fixable — provided you act before you start playing and before the deposit ages out of the window support can work with. This guide walks through why bonuses fail to credit, what to check yourself, and how to escalate effectively when the problem is on the room's side. If you are still choosing an offer, it is worth favouring rooms with a track record of crediting reliably in the first place.

The first thing worth saying is that a missing bonus is usually not a scam. In the overwhelming majority of cases it is a mechanical failure — a code entered in the wrong field, a payment method excluded from the promotion, or a pending balance that exists but is displayed somewhere the player has not looked. The rooms have no interest in withholding a bonus they advertised; an unhappy first-time depositor is an expensive customer to lose.

Step one: check whether it actually is missing

More missing bonuses turn out to be present than you might expect. A poker bonus almost never lands in your cashier balance as spendable money. It sits in a separate pending or locked pool and drips into your real balance as you generate rake, which means the place to look is not the number at the top of the client.

  • Look in the promotions or bonus tab of the client, not the cashier. Most rooms show pending bonus, amount released so far, and time remaining there.
  • Check the web account area as well as the desktop client. The two are not always in sync, and some rooms only display bonus progress in one of them.
  • Restart the client. A surprising share of 'missing' bonuses appear after a full logout and login, because the balance was credited server-side after the session started.
  • Give crypto deposits time to confirm. The bonus attaches when the deposit confirms on-chain, not when you send it — that can be minutes or, at busy times, considerably longer.

If the deposit itself has not landed, the bonus question is premature. Confirm the funds are in your account first, then worry about the match.

The usual reasons a bonus fails to credit

Assuming the deposit cleared and there is genuinely no pending bonus, the cause is almost always one of the following.

  1. The bonus code was not entered, or was entered in the wrong place. Many rooms have separate code fields at registration and in the cashier, and they are not interchangeable. A code that belongs at signup will silently do nothing if pasted into a deposit screen.
  2. The deposit method is excluded. Certain promotions — particularly enhanced crypto offers — apply only to specific funding methods. Depositing by card on a Bitcoin-only promotion will fund your account perfectly and credit no bonus at all.
  3. The deposit was below the minimum. Almost every offer has a floor, often somewhere around twenty to fifty dollars. A deposit of less than that qualifies for nothing, and the client rarely warns you at the time.
  4. The account already used a welcome offer. Welcome bonuses are one per player, and rooms match on more than just email address. A previous account, even a dormant one, will block the offer.
  5. A different promotion was already active. Some rooms allow only one live bonus at a time, so depositing while a reload is still clearing means the new bonus is declined rather than queued.
  6. Verification is incomplete. Where a room requires identity checks before promotional credit, an unverified account may receive the deposit but hold the bonus until documents clear.

Running through that list honestly resolves the majority of cases, and it matters because the fix differs completely depending on the cause. A code entered in the wrong field is usually recoverable by support. A deposit made on an excluded payment method often is not, because the promotion's terms were never satisfied. Our how to claim walkthrough covers the sequence that avoids most of these problems in the first place — read the terms, enter the code at the stated point, meet the minimum, deposit by a qualifying method.

What to do before you contact support

There is one piece of advice that matters more than everything else here: stop playing. If you deposited expecting a bonus and it did not credit, do not sit down at a table while you sort it out.

The reason is practical. Most rooms can retroactively apply a missing bonus to a deposit, but only if the deposited funds are still substantially intact. Once you have played through the balance, the deposit that the bonus was supposed to match no longer exists in any meaningful sense, and support's usual answer becomes a polite refusal. A player who contacts support within an hour of an untouched deposit has a strong position. A player who contacts them three days and two thousand hands later has almost none.

While you wait, gather the details that will make the conversation short: the exact deposit amount, the date and time, the payment method (with the transaction hash if it was crypto), the bonus code you used, and where you found the offer. A screenshot of the promotion page as it appeared to you is worth more than any amount of description, particularly if the terms have since changed.

Contacting support effectively

Live chat is almost always the fastest route, and it is available around the clock at the offshore rooms serving US and Canadian players. Email creates a better paper trail but can take a day or more, which is a problem given how much the timing matters.

Open with the facts in one message rather than drip-feeding them. Something along the lines of: deposited $200 by Bitcoin at this time on this date, entered code X at the cashier as instructed on your promotions page, deposit credited correctly, no pending bonus visible in the promotions tab after a client restart, funds untouched. That gives the agent everything needed to check the account without a round of questions, and it signals that you know how the process works.

Be civil and specific. Front-line agents rarely have authority to grant a bonus outright, but they can see your account history, confirm whether the code registered, and escalate to a promotions team that can. Hostility does not speed that up; a clean, factual summary does. If the first agent tells you the offer does not apply, ask them to explain which specific term was not met. That answer is either informative — in which case you have learned something — or vague, which is grounds to escalate politely to a supervisor or follow up by email.

When the answer is no

Sometimes the bonus genuinely was not earned, and it is worth recognising that outcome rather than fighting it. If you deposited by an excluded method or below the minimum, the terms were not satisfied and no amount of escalation will change that. Many rooms will offer a goodwill alternative in those situations — a smaller bonus, a tournament ticket, or permission to apply the offer to your next deposit — and accepting one is usually better value than an argument you will not win.

Where the room's own error is clear and it still declines, that is useful information about the operator. A room that advertises an offer, accepts a deposit against it and then refuses to honour or explain it has told you what its customer service is like before you had any real money at risk. The review we maintain for Americas Cardroom and the other rooms we cover weighs support responsiveness alongside the headline numbers, because a bonus you cannot actually collect is not worth anything.

Avoiding the problem next time

The habits that prevent this are short and dull, which is probably why so few players follow them. Read the full terms before depositing rather than the banner. Screenshot the offer page. Note where the code goes and enter it there. Confirm the minimum and the eligible payment methods. Then, after the deposit confirms, check the promotions tab before you open a single table — the two minutes that takes is the difference between a fixable problem and an unfixable one.

Compare offers on the terms that determine whether you will actually receive the money rather than on the headline percentage, and you will run into this situation far less often. Our bonus types guide explains how each structure credits and releases, and the best poker bonuses page ranks current offers on that basis. If a bonus has already gone missing, close the client, gather your details, and open a chat before you play another hand — that single decision determines how this ends.

Frequently asked questions

Can a poker room add a bonus after I have already deposited?

Often yes, if you contact support quickly and the deposited funds are untouched. Once you have played through the deposit, retroactive crediting is usually refused.

Why did my deposit credit but my bonus did not?

The most common causes are a code entered in the wrong field, a payment method excluded from the promotion, or a deposit below the offer's stated minimum.
